# Inquiry Reference Item Internals

## Product Value

The GM should be able to notice and consult intentionally prepared discovery material when players seek information, without requiring every possible inquiry check to be authored in advance.

## Goal

Prototype the internal grammar of an [inquiry reference](../vocabulary.md#inquiry-reference) and produce the first candidate visual language for compact T1 identity with richer T2 resolution.

Use the [shared prototype guidance](../reminder-inquiry-shared-guidance.md) for common constraints.

## Questions To Test

- What does the GM scan for first: exact mechanical action, inquiry type (i.e., manual/visual/magical), target, object, skill, or fictional context?
- Can action + target form a short, discriminating T1 line, or was that shape mostly an Obsidian-era constraint?
- Can target-first rows with inquiry-type icons scan better than action-first rows?
- Does T1 need skill, DC, or a compact type mark, or do those details damage scanning?
- How should T2 group skill, DC parts, adjustments, disclosed facts, degree-of-success outcomes, and explicit deeper references?
- Which parts of PF2e statblock grammar help T2 resolution: traits, named sections, trigger/requirements/effect order, degree-of-success blocks, action icons, or source links?
- Do indented inline details, expansion, and adjacent detail treatments require different widths or preserve orientation differently?
- How should closely related checks, such as the restroom rune checks, remain distinguishable while sharing explicit references to common context?
- Can the item remain visibly lower-obligation than a reminder if later placed in the same surface?
- Does the current shape survive mixed discovery/manipulation or consequence-bearing samples, or should those remain free-form procedure notes until they develop their own runtime format?

## Representative Samples

- `Вспомнить информацию о костях`
- `Идентифицировать магию на двери и в рунах`
- `Идентифицировать магию в рунах над дверью`
- `Идентифицировать магию в рунах на потолке`
- `Искать на ковре, или потратить время на его очистку` as a boundary sample

Include the shared `Рунная магия Пинь Тяньгу` reference, but preserve it as an explicit link or locally authored adjustment summary. Do not automatically combine Understanding Points with the local DC.

## Alternatives

Compare at least:

1. Action + target as T1, preserving the vault's strongest existing pattern.
2. Target-first T1 with an inquiry-type icon, keeping exact mechanical action in T2.
3. A row that keeps a small amount of roll context in T1.
4. A statblock-like T2 treatment using traits, named sections, degree-of-success blocks, or source links where they help.
5. An adjacent or popup-like detail treatment that preserves the compact row while exposing the complete check.

## Out Of Scope

- Exhaustive authoring of possible inquiries.
- Generic PF2e Recall Knowledge, Search, Seek, or Identify Magic reference UX.
- Automatic DC calculation or cross-reference composition.
- Domain modeling around inquirable scene objects or effects.
- The final decision to combine inquiry references with reminders.
- The contextual reference browser.

## Expected Outcomes

- Credible T1 and T2 treatments.
- Width and wrapping evidence.
- Guidance for preserving authored DC context, including base/by-level or shape DC, difficulty/rarity adjustments, and local modifiers.
- Guidance for preserving authored inquiry identity if the prototype chooses target-first, icon-led, or another non-action-first T1 display.
- Provisional authoring guidance for explicit inquiry-reference identity.
- Evidence for or against the current action-and-target structured specialization.
