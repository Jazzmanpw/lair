# Reminder And Inquiry Surface Composition

## Dependencies

Begin only after the inquiry-reference, environmental-reminder, creature-interrupt, and custom-reminder item studies satisfy the [prototype index's list-readiness criteria](./reminder-inquiry-item-prototypes.md#list-readiness).

Use the [shared prototype guidance](../reminder-inquiry-shared-guidance.md) for common constraints.

## Product Value

The GM needs a focused reminder set without paying excessive navigation cost for intentionally prepared inquiry material. The surface must preserve the different weight of “this consequence should happen” and “this prepared discovery material can help.”

## Goal

Compare list and surface composition after item internals are understood.

## Required Alternatives

1. Reminder-only list: compose obligation reminders and leave inquiry references outside this surface.
2. One mixed list: combine reminder rows and visibly lower-weight inquiry-reference rows in the same list.
3. Two neighboring surfaces: keep reminder and inquiry lists structurally separate, but place them close enough to scan as one reminder/inquiry area.

Compare neutral rows, compact marks, and provisional color treatments. The initial color hypothesis is blue-ish for persistent effects, sand for environmental triggers, warm gold or red-orange for custom reminders, and muted green for inquiry references, but the list task owns whether color survives mixed-density testing.

Later Running Mode layout work is the final trial for the number and placement of surfaces.

## Questions To Test

- Can mixed T1 rows remain scannable at representative density?
- Does lower inquiry weight remain readable and discoverable without competing with consequences?
- Which ordering, compact marks, or grouping communicate role without making taxonomy the main content?
- Do pending triggers, active effects, and creature interrupts need internal grouping?
- How do custom reminder notes scan beside structured reminders?
- How should exploration and tactics reprioritize content without hiding valid reminders?
- How do keep, dismiss, recovery, and stale-source treatment affect focus?
- What aggregate behavior belongs to a lens, filter, sort, or expandable group?
- How much width and height does each credible alternative require?

## Guardrails

- Do not casually rediscover item internals inside list composition. If composition exposes a small internal mismatch, record the adjustment and link it back to the item task; if it exposes a major uncertainty, pause composition and run a focused follow-up for that candidate before returning.
- Do not split by exploration and tactics merely because a reminder's priority changes.
- Keep de-emphasized and dismissed information recoverable.
- Treat grouping and type marks as tools to compare, not a taxonomy to canonize.

## Expected Outcomes

- One or more composition directions ready for layout work.
- Density and dimension evidence.
- A clear account of which distinctions require visual weight, structural separation, or only ordering.
- Deferred keyboard, focus, and full-layout questions stated for the next task.
