# Running Mode Vocabulary

## Common abbreviations

- PC - Player Character.
- SoC - Source of Conflict. A reason why a Dramatic Question (what PCs try to achieve) can't be answered.
- A/M - Aspect or Motivation. Properties of session participants that describe their desires and intentions. They create SoCs when oppose PCs' goals.

## Lens

A lens preserves the current layout while changing or augmenting information in place, as if the GM put on special glasses.

- Existing elements may replace their normal content with another view.
- Related elements may highlight, annotate, or reveal additional information.
- Tooltips may count as part of a lens when they augment the existing surface.
- The underlying spatial structure remains recognizable and stable.

A drawer is not a lens merely because it is temporary. It becomes part of a lens only when it meaningfully changes or interacts with the content already on the page.

## Tab

A tab switches to a distinct content surface and is free to use a different internal shape.

An all-tabs design and an all-lenses design may both reduce always-visible content, but they make different spatial promises:

- lenses preserve the layout and reinterpret it;
- tabs preserve a navigation position while replacing the active surface.

## Situational Rule

A situational rule is the current name for a free-form prep-note usage that provides additional context or mechanics for something that may happen. It can relate to one scene, several scenes, an adventure, or other prepared content; it is not owned by a scene merely because that scene makes it relevant.

The term does not imply that every situational rule is a formal game-system rule or a physical object. The [Building Mode content authoring guidance](../building-mode/content-authoring-guidance-draft.md#situational-rules) owns its content role and relationship to more structured prep units.

## Inquiry Reference

An inquiry reference is an authored prep note the GM can consult when players seek information through a check. It points to roll-guarded discovery content without implying that every possible inquiry is prepared in advance.

Its authored identity currently records an inquiry action and target, while its in-app T1 identity remains a prototype question. Its resolution content contains the roll mechanics and the facts disclosed by its effects. The [Building Mode content authoring guidance](../building-mode/content-authoring-guidance-draft.md#inquiry-references) owns its authoring shape and expected coverage.
