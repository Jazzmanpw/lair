# Running Mode Vocabulary

## Lens

A lens preserves the current layout while changing or augmenting information in place, as if the GM put on special glasses.

- Existing elements may replace their normal content with another view.
- Related elements may highlight, annotate, or reveal additional information.
- Tooltips may count as part of a lens when they augment the existing surface.
- The underlying spatial structure remains recognizable and stable.

A drawer is not a lens merely because it is temporary. It becomes part of a lens only when it meaningfully changes or interacts with the content already on the page.

## Tab

A tab switches to a distinct content surface and is free to use a different internal shape.

An all-tabs design and an all-lenses design may both reduce always-visible content, but they make different spatial promises:

- lenses preserve the layout and reinterpret it;
- tabs preserve a navigation position while replacing the active surface.
